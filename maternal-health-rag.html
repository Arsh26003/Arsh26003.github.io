A RAG assistant over the WHO antenatal care guidelines

A retrieval-augmented generation assistant built over the WHO's antenatal care guideline document — the first of several hands-on Health AI projects exploring maternal and prenatal health, an area that's underserved by existing tooling.

Health AI · Skill-building project

The setup

The pipeline loads the WHO Antenatal Care Guidelines PDF from page 13 onward (skipping frontmatter), splits it into roughly 689 chunks with a 1000-character window and 200-character overlap, embeds each chunk locally with sentence-transformers' all-MiniLM-L6-v2, and stores the result in a local Chroma vector database — LangChain tying the pipeline together.

Where it stands

  • Full pipeline is built and working end to end, with checkpoints committed to GitHub.
  • Testing retrieval against real queries surfaced two gaps worth fixing: vocabulary mismatches around antenatal-care terminology, and a genuine hole in the source document around danger signs during pregnancy.

Coming next

  • Close the vocabulary-mismatch gap so queries phrased in plain language still hit the right chunks.
  • Work out how to handle the danger-signs content gap in the source material itself.
  • Use this project as a proving ground for which AI subfield — AI Engineering, ML/Data Engineering, or Applied ML — to go deeper on next.
